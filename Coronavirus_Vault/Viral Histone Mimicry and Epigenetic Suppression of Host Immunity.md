Based on the sources provided, the following list identifies the types of viruses, the specific histone marks and their codes, and the related functional or biological annotations.

### Types of Viruses

* **SARS-CoV-2:** The primary virus analyzed across most sources, responsible for COVID-19 1-3.  
* **MERS-CoV (Middle East Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus):** Highly pathogenic coronavirus used to study ISG suppression 4, 5\.  
* **SARS-CoV (Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus):** The virus from the 2002/2003 outbreak 6, 7\.  
* **HCoV-229E (Human Coronavirus 229E):** Used as a model to study alterations in intact histone proteoforms 8, 9\.

### Histone Marks and Functional Annotations

Type of Mark,Mark Code,Virus,Functional / Biological Annotation  
Methylation,"H3K9me3, H3K27me3","SARS-CoV-2, MERS-CoV","Transcriptional Repression / Gene Silencing: ORF8 acts as a histone mimic to increase these marks, promoting chromatin compaction and silencing antiviral genes 1, 10-13."  
Methylation,H3K4me3,"SARS-CoV-2, SARS-CoV","Transcriptional Activation: Found at promoters of highly active genes; depleted at ISG promoters in MERS-CoV to prevent an antiviral state 10, 13-16."  
Methylation,H3K9me2,SARS-CoV-2,"Repressive Mark: Inversely correlates with interferon expression; used by the virus to inhibit the innate immune response 17, 18."  
Acetylation,"H3K9ac, H3K14ac",SARS-CoV-2,"Transcriptional Activation: Necessary to mount a potent antiviral state; decreased by ORF8 to weaken the host's interferon (IFN) response 10, 19, 20."  
Acetylation,H3K27ac,SARS-CoV-2,"Regulation of ACE2: Associated with the activation of ACE2-related genes, impacting viral entry 21-23."  
Acetylation,H2AX-K6ac,HCoV-229E,DNA Damage Response: Regulates H2AX release and chromatin reorganization after DNA double-strand breaks; decreased abundance during infection 24\.  
Citrullination,Cit-H3 (Citrullinated H3),SARS-CoV-2,"NETosis Marker: Indicates neutrophil extracellular trap formation; elevated levels drive vascular damage, ARDS, and predict 28-day mortality 25-27."  
Phosphorylation,H2AC11-T17ph,HCoV-229E,Oxidative Stress: Identified as a specific marker for virus-induced oxidative stress in human lung fibroblasts 24\.  
Phosphorylation,H3S10ph,SARS-CoV-2,Cell Cycle / Activation: Mentioned as a mark monitored during ORF8 expression studies 28\.  
Glutathionylation,H3C1 (at C97/C111),HCoV-229E,Structural Destabilization: Known to destabilize the nucleosome complex 29\.  
Truncation (Clipping),C-terminally truncated H2A,HCoV-229E,"Structural Regulation: Truncation normally destabilizes nucleosomes and removes PTM sites; abundance is reduced during infection 8, 30, 31."  
Truncation (Clipping),N-terminally truncated H3,HCoV-229E,"Structural Regulation: Cleaved at R27 or K28; abundance reduced during infection to potentially modulate DNA accessibility 8, 32."

### Additional PTMs Identified via Proteomics

The sources identify a broad range of modifications associated with infection, many of whose specific functional roles in the viral life cycle are still being elucidated:

* **Acetylation:** H3K18ac, H3K23ac, H2AK5ac 33\.  
* **Methylation:** H3K4me1, H3K36me3, H3.3K27me3, H4K20me1 33\.  
* **HCoV-229E Specific Observations:** Methylation at H4R20, H4K21, H4R24, H4R68, H3K28, H3K37, H3R50, H3K57, H3K80, and H3R84 29, 34\.
